# Early Game

The early game covers Act 1: Founder Operator.

Act 1 should teach the player the core identity of the game without overwhelming them.

The player starts with a tiny independent air service, one small aircraft, limited money, and minimal credibility. The airline is not a real scheduled carrier yet. It is a fragile founder-run operation trying to prove it can survive.

Act 1 should take some time, but a focused player should be able to complete it in a day.

## Early Game Design Goals

Act 1 should:

- Start extremely simple
- Introduce mechanics one at a time
- Make the player feel like a founder
- Teach that aircraft choice matters
- Teach that scheduling matters
- Teach that money and reliability matter
- Introduce story objectives
- Introduce optional contracts
- Introduce operations catch-up
- Prepare the player for scheduled commuter operations

Act 1 should not overwhelm the player with the full simulator immediately.

## Starting State

At the start of Act 1, the player chooses:

- Airline name
- Starting airport
- Starter aircraft package
- Difficulty
- Simulation pace

The player begins with:

- One small aircraft
- Limited cash
- Very limited route authority
- Minimal reputation
- No partner airline
- No large airport access
- No advanced business systems

## Starting Aircraft Choice

The player should choose between two starter aircraft.

The choice should teach that aircraft are strategic decisions.

Example starter options:

- Aster A-8 Courier: cheaper, lower capacity, lower comfort, lower reliability
- Kestrel K-10 Trail: more expensive, higher capacity, better rugged airport performance, better reliability

Both options should be viable.

## Feature Unlock Philosophy

The game should not show every system immediately.

Act 1 should unlock basic features through story objectives.

The player should receive new tools as the airline becomes more capable.

## Initial Unlocked Features

At the very beginning, the player should only need:

- Airline setup
- Home airport selection
- Starter aircraft selection
- Basic dashboard
- Inbox
- Simplified route planning
- Simplified schedule board
- Pause/resume controls using the selected save pace
- Simple operations report after the first flight or operating period

## Act 1 Feature Unlocks

At the start of Act 1, the player has access to:

- Basic dashboard
- Inbox
- Simplified route planning
- Simplified schedule board
- Pause/resume controls using the selected save pace

Act 1 then unlocks additional systems in sequence:

1. Operations report
2. Reliability tracking
3. Operations catch-up
4. Private contracts
5. Business/finance view
6. Aircraft condition and maintenance view
7. Expanded route planning
8. Second route permission
9. Commuter certification path

## Feature Unlock Rule

Features should unlock before the objective that requires them.

During Act 1, the game may begin with simplified versions of core tools and later unlock more detailed versions.

For example, the player may start with simplified route planning and a simplified schedule board. Later objectives unlock deeper route analysis, operations reports, contracts, finance, maintenance, and expanded route permissions.

## Act 1 Limits

Act 1 should allow freedom but prevent unlimited scaling.

The player can operate, optimize, take side contracts, and earn money, but larger expansion requires story progression.

Possible Act 1 limits:

- Limited number of scheduled routes
- Limited number of aircraft
- Limited airport classes
- Limited aircraft categories
- No partner contracts
- No large regional aircraft
- No full hub mechanics
- No advanced finance systems

These limits represent the airline's early legal, operational, and credibility constraints.

## Revised Act 1 Feature Flow

### Starting Setup

The player creates the airline, chooses a starting airport, and chooses a starter aircraft.

Unlocked after setup:

- Simplified route planning
- Simplified schedule board
- Basic dashboard
- Inbox

### Objective 1: Pick a First Route

The player chooses one eligible route from their starting airport.

Required feature:

- Simplified route planning

Unlocks after completion:

- First schedule objective
- Co-founder guidance about keeping the first route simple

### Objective 2: Build the First Schedule

The player schedules at least one round trip.

Unlocks after completion:

- First operating period
- Co-founder guidance about running the clock

### Objective 3: Run the First Operating Period

The player resumes the simulation clock and lets the first scheduled flight or operating period complete.

Required feature:

- Simulation controls

Unlocks after completion:

- Operations report
- Route performance summary

### Objective 4: Review the First Report

The player reviews the operations report from the first day.

Required feature:

- Operations report

Unlocks after completion:

- Multi-day operation
- Basic reliability tracking

### Objective 5: Make It Repeatable

The player operates several simulated days with no canceled flights.

Required features:

- Selected save pace
- Basic reliability tracking

Unlocks after completion:

- Operations catch-up

### Objective 6: Let the Schedule Run

The player uses operations catch-up or simulates multiple days from the same schedule.

Required feature:

- Operations catch-up

Unlocks after completion:

- Private contracts
- Contract inbox messages

### Objective 7: Take Work Where You Can Get It

The player accepts and completes a small private contract.

Required feature:

- Private contracts

Unlocks after completion:

- Business/finance view

### Objective 8: Watch the Numbers

The player reviews revenue, costs, and cash flow.

Required feature:

- Business/finance view

Unlocks after completion:

- Aircraft condition and maintenance view

### Objective 9: Keep the Aircraft Healthy

The player handles or acknowledges aircraft maintenance needs.

Required feature:

- Maintenance view

Unlocks after completion:

- Second route permission
- Expanded route planning

### Objective 10: Prove Scheduled Reliability

The player operates a small schedule with 1-2 routes while meeting reliability and cash requirements.

Required features:

- Expanded route planning
- Schedule board
- Maintenance view
- Business/finance view

Unlocks after completion:

- Commuter certification application

### Objective 11: Apply for Scheduled Commuter Status

The player chooses whether to continue operating in Act 1 or apply for Scheduled Commuter status.

Required feature:

- Certification path

Unlocks after completion:

- Act 2 transition

## Private Contracts

Private contracts are optional early side work.

They represent small opportunities that help the player earn money and learn operations.

Examples:

- Weekend event shuttle
- Community airport trial service
- Medical travel support
- Small business shuttle
- College town game-day route
- Tourism weekend package
- Work crew transport

Private contracts should be small, grounded, and useful.

They should not feel like random chores.

## Co-Founder / Advisor

The co-founder or advisor is the player’s main guide in the early game.

This character helps explain mechanics, introduce objectives, warn about risks, and provide story context.

The advisor should feel like a practical partner, not a cartoon tutorial character.

Tone:

- Friendly
- Grounded
- Practical
- Brief
- Occasionally encouraging
- Focused on the airline's survival and growth

The advisor can appear through inbox messages, objective notes, reports, and strategic decision panels.

## Inbox System

The inbox is the main delivery system for story, contracts, business messages, and operational updates.

The inbox keeps story integrated into the simulator interface without forcing constant popups.

Message types may include:

- Story
- Contracts
- Operations
- Finance
- Airport
- Partner
- System

Messages may include:

- Sender
- Subject
- Short body
- Related objective
- Related contract
- Action button
- Reward or unlock information

## Inbox Examples

### Story Message

Sender: Co-Founder  
Subject: First route, first risk  
Body: One aircraft means one bad schedule can ruin the day. Start with something short, repeatable, and boring enough to survive.

Action: Open Route Planning

### Operations Message

Sender: Dispatch  
Subject: First day complete  
Body: The first day is in the books. Review the report before adding more flying.

Action: Open Operations Report

### Contract Message

Sender: Community Airport Office  
Subject: Weekend shuttle request  
Body: A nearby community needs short-term passenger service this weekend. It is not glamorous, but it pays.

Action: View Contract

## Act 1 Completion

Act 1 ends when the player proves the airline can operate reliably enough to pursue scheduled commuter status.

The player should not be forced to transition immediately.

The game should present the transition as a choice:

- Continue operating as a founder service
- Apply for Scheduled Commuter status

The player controls the pace.
