# Vision

This game is an open-world airline career simulator.

The player begins as the founder of a tiny independent air service with one small aircraft, limited money, and almost no credibility. Through story-driven career objectives, optional contracts, aircraft progression, route planning, scheduling, and business decisions, the player grows from a founder-run operation into a scheduled commuter carrier, regional partner airline, independent regional airline, national carrier, and eventually a larger aviation group.

The game is a mashup between a simulator and an open-world progression story game.

The simulator is the world. The airline is the player character. Routes, aircraft, airports, contracts, partnerships, finances, and reputation are the tools for progression.

The main storyline gives the player direction, but it does not lock them into a fixed path. Players can follow the main career path, stay in a stage longer, complete side contracts, build extra routes, save money, optimize operations, or prepare for future expansion before progressing.

Story progression happens when the player is actively playing. Operations catch-up runs stable scheduled operations while the player is away, but it does not trigger major story decisions automatically.

The game is designed to be long-form. It should not be something the player finishes in a weekend. The goal is to slowly learn, build, struggle, and grow from a tiny operation into a serious airline.
